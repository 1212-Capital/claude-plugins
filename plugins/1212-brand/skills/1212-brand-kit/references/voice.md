# 1212 Capital — voice and copy

## Positioning

> **Anchored in traditional finance. Fluent in digital asset markets.**

Supporting lines already approved and in use:

- Clarity, compounded, since 2022.
- Digital-asset strategies built for clarity. Weekly NAV, full transparency.
- Uncorrelated by design.
- Your capital, compounding quietly.
- Liquid, risk-managed mandates with independent custody.

## Tone

Editorial, precise, unhurried. The brand sells calm in a loud market, so the
copy never shouts. Concretely:

- **Claim, then evidence.** "Uncorrelated by design" is followed by
  "1212.Stable held its line while BTC drew down nearly 30%." A claim with no
  figure behind it does not ship.
- **Figures carry the emphasis, not adjectives.** Drop "exceptional",
  "outstanding", "incredible". If the number is good it does the work.
- **Short sentences. One idea each.** Two clauses maximum.
- **Say what a thing is before saying why it matters.** Definitions first,
  argument second. This is why the fact sheet carries a glossary.
- **Name the risk.** Every product statement is paired with its limitation.
  "Stablecoins are not risk-free." "Non-directional does not mean risk-free."
  This is house style and also the compliance posture.

## Mechanics

- **No em dashes.** Ever. Use a period or a comma. Applies to every surface.
- Middle dot `·` separates meta items: `1212.STABLE · FACT SHEET · AS OF 30 JUNE 2026`.
- Meta lines, eyebrows and kickers are UPPERCASE mono with letter-spacing.
- Section labels are Sentence-free UPPERCASE inside the lilac tag.
- Headlines are sentence case, not title case: "Bitcoin falls below $60,000".
- Dates: `DD MMMM YYYY` in prose and meta lines, `DD/MM/YYYY` in key-fact
  tables, `MMM YYYY` in month columns.
- Percentages carry two decimals in the fact sheet (`12.19%`), one or none in
  marketing (`+7.0%`, `30%`).
- Currency: `$XX,XXX`, `$X.XX T` for trillions.
- Products are written `1212.Stable`, never "1212 Stable" or "the 1212 fund".
- English throughout, including internal documents.

## Compliance register

Every externally distributed document carries, at minimum:

- "Confidential. For professional investors only. Not for retail distribution."
  on the cover.
- "Past performance is not indicative of future results." in the legal block.
- A named source and as-of date beneath every table and every chart.

Internal documents carry "Internal document. Not for distribution outside 1212
Capital." and a footer reading `1212 CAPITAL · CONFIDENTIAL · NOT FOR
DISTRIBUTION`.

Do not invent regulatory status, entity names or registration numbers. Those
ship as bracketed placeholders and are filled by counsel.

## Words to avoid

guaranteed · risk-free · safe · secure returns · passive income (unqualified) ·
disruptive · revolutionary · unlock · leverage (as a verb) · best-in-class ·
we are thrilled / excited / proud.
