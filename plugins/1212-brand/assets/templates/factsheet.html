GLOSSARY
Stablecoin
A digital asset designed to maintain a reference value, typically pegged to a fiat currency such as the U.S. dollar. Stability is not guaranteed. Risks include depegging, issuer risk, collateral risk, liquidity risk, and regulatory risk.
Collateralization / Verifiable Collateral
The mechanism by which a stablecoin is backed by reserve assets. “Verifiable collateral” refers to disclosed or attested reserves but does not constitute a guarantee against loss.
Non-Directional Strategy
An investment approach whose performance is not intended to depend on upward or downward movements of volatile crypto-assets. Non-directional does not mean risk-free.
DeFi (Decentralized Finance)
A financial ecosystem built on blockchain networks using smart contracts to facilitate lending, borrowing, trading, and yield generation without traditional intermediaries.
On-Chain
Transactions and positions executed and recorded directly on a blockchain, accessible through public ledgers. On-chain transparency does not eliminate operational or market risks.
Liquidity Providing / AMM (Automated Market Maker)
The provision of assets to decentralized liquidity pools to facilitate trading, in exchange for fees and/or incentives. Risks include pool imbalance, smart contract failure, and changing incentive structures.
Incentives
Rewards distributed by protocols (often in native tokens) to encourage participation. Incentives may fluctuate, be discontinued, or lose value and are not guaranteed.
Lending / Borrowing
On-chain markets enabling users to lend assets to earn yield or borrow assets against collateral. Risks include liquidation risk, protocol risk, oracle risk, and liquidity constraints.
Fixed Yield / Principal Tokens
Structures designed to lock in a predetermined yield over a defined period. Risks include pricing risk, secondary market liquidity risk, protocol risk, and execution risk.
Multisignature Wallet
A wallet requiring multiple approvals to execute transactions. While it reduces single-key risk, it introduces governance and coordination risks.
RISK CONSIDERATIONS
Market & valuation
Digital asset markets can move sharply and valuations may be uncertain, particularly for assets with limited secondary liquidity.
Stablecoin depeg
Stablecoins are not risk-free and may deviate from their intended reference value, temporarily or permanently.
Liquidity
Positions may not be realizable at short notice, or at carrying value, during periods of market stress.
Counterparty & custody
The strategy depends on protocols, venues and custody arrangements whose failure could impair or delay recovery of positions.
Smart contract & protocol
Code may contain vulnerabilities. Exploits, governance failures, oracle failures and network congestion can each cause loss.
Regulatory
The treatment of digital assets and decentralized finance is evolving and may change adversely, including with retroactive effect.
Operational
Execution, reconciliation and key management processes carry the risk of human or system error.
